About

Ancient Faith, Latter-day Questions.

A public-facing exploration of Scripture, early Christianity, historical theology, and the Restoration.

Why this podcast?

Ancient Faith, Latter-day Questions exists at the intersection of biblical studies, early Christian history, and Latter-day Saint theology. Each episode begins with a historical or theological question and follows the evidence through Scripture, ancient sources, and modern scholarship.

The Restoration provides the perspective, but not necessarily the conclusion. The goal is to understand ancient Christianity on its own terms and then ask what that history might mean for Latter-day Saints today.

A few guiding principles

  • Primary sources first. Whenever possible, begin with the texts themselves.
  • Evidence before apologetics. A good argument should survive contact with inconvenient evidence.
  • Fair representation. Serious alternatives deserve to be presented in their strongest form.
  • Intellectual humility. “We don't know” is sometimes the most responsible conclusion.

About the host

Zach Stainback is a disciple of Christ, husband to Rylee, and father to two adorable children. A fifth generaiton member of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints with no ties to Utah, Zach grew up in the Bible Belt: rural eastern North Carolina to be exact. After serving a Spanish-speaking mission in southern Utah from 2014-2016, Zach bounced back and forth between Utah and North Carolina for several years, pursuing various educational and vocational options along the way. He started at BYU studying political science while working part-time at a grocery store in Heber City. After returning to North Carolina, Zach moved into a management position with Piggly Wiggly grocery stores while working finishing his bachelors online at BYU-Idaho, this time in Marriage and Family Studies. Another move provided the opportunity to test out career paths in mental health, which he quickly found were not what he wanted. He would later study computer science and web devleopment just enough to know how to piece together a website before deciding to double down on his business experience. When Piggly Wiggly reached out with an offer to become a store manager, Zach and Rylee moved their family back to North Carolina, where Zach would determine that management was not what he wanted to do long term either. After some time and prayer, he decided to follow his passion and apply to a degree focused on biblical studies. The Master of Theology in New Testament and Early Christianity offered online by the University of Aberdeen was the perfect fit. Zach started the program in 2026. He quickly realized that there is much to early Christian beliefs and texts with which most Latter-day Saints have had little to no engagement. Zach believes that there is immense value in studying the beliefs, teachings, and writings of early Christians, and he is anxious to share how an informed Latter-day Saint perspective can contribute to meaningful discussion of ancient Christianity.
