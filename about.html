About

Ancient questions. Latter-day questions.

A public-facing exploration of Scripture, early Christianity, historical theology, and the Restoration.

Why this podcast?

Ancient Faith, Latter-day Questions exists at the intersection of biblical studies, early Christian history, and Latter-day Saint theology. Each episode begins with a historical or theological question and follows the evidence through Scripture, ancient sources, and modern scholarship.

The Restoration provides the perspective, but not necessarily the conclusion. The goal is to understand ancient Christianity on its own terms and then ask what that history might mean for Latter-day Saints today.

A few guiding principles

  • Primary sources first. Whenever possible, begin with the texts themselves.
  • Evidence before apologetics. A good argument should survive contact with inconvenient evidence.
  • Fair representation. Serious alternatives deserve to be presented in their strongest form.
  • Intellectual humility. “We don't know” is sometimes the most responsible conclusion.
