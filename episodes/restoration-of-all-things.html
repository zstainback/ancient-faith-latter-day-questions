What Did Peter Mean by “The Restoration of All Things”?

Acts 3:19–21 · apokatastasis · Elijah · Early Christian restoration

“Whom the heaven must receive until the times of restitution of all things…”

The question

Peter's words in Acts 3:21 have generated centuries of theological debate. What exactly is being restored? What did Peter's language mean in its Jewish and early Christian context? And how should Latter-day Saints think about the relationship between this ancient expectation and the Restoration?

Episode outline

  • The Greek term apokatastasis
  • Restoration expectations in Jewish Scripture
  • Malachi, Elijah, and Acts
  • Early Christian interpretations
  • Joseph Smith and Restoration theology
  • What the evidence can—and cannot—establish
